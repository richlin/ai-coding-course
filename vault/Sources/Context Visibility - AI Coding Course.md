# Context Visibility — AI Coding Course

## Summary
Frames a harness's token-usage/context indicators as a warning signal about context pressure, not a direct measurement of answer quality — the deciding evidence is behavior (repeated questions, forgotten constraints), not the percentage itself.

## Key takeaways
- Rising context usage warns that old information may be crowding the active task; it doesn't by itself confirm degraded output.
- Unused context space remaining does not mean the session is healthy — a session can degrade at low reported usage if noise is disproportionate.
- Keep a current-state artifact independent of the status indicator, and reconfirm constraints after automatic compaction rather than trusting the indicator alone.
- Learn what your specific harness counts toward usage (tool output, hidden instructions) — visibility tools differ by harness.
