# Conductor vs Orchestrator Modes

Two modes of directing a coding agent, distinguished by interaction cadence.

## The distinction

**Conductor mode** — real-time, IDE-based interaction. The person stays in the loop turn by turn, similar to [[Active Partnership]]'s shared-investigation pattern.

**Orchestrator mode** — async goal-delegation. The person hands off a goal and checks back later, trading turn-by-turn steering for throughput.

## Why it matters

The two modes carry different risk profiles: conductor mode catches a bad direction early at the cost of the person's attention; orchestrator mode is cheaper in attention but can run further down a wrong path before anyone notices — raising the value of [[Acceptance Checks]] as the safety net when no one is watching turn-by-turn.
