# Q-006 — How do teams close the 80% problem?

## Question
How do teams systematically close the gap between an agent's fast initial functionality and the edge cases and system-integration seams that resist the same speed?

## Why it matters
If the gap is only closed case-by-case, the "implementation is now cheap" gain from [[Lifecycle Compression]] is overstated — the remaining 20% may dominate total time and cost. See [[Total Cost of AI Development]].
