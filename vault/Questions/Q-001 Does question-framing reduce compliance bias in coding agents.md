# Q-001 — Does question-framing reduce compliance bias in coding agents?

The "Ask, Don't Tell" study ([arxiv.org/abs/2602.23971](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23971)) found substantially less expressed sycophancy when content was presented as a question rather than a confident statement. The study was not about coding tasks.

**The open question:** Does this transfer reliably to engineering-context prompting, where the agent is also reading code, running tools, and producing concrete artifacts — not just generating prose responses?
