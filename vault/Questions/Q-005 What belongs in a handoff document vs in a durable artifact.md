# Q-005 — What belongs in a handoff document vs. in a durable artifact?

The statelessness lesson says to "link to durable artifacts instead of copying their full contents." But the boundary is fuzzy: a decision rationale could belong in the handoff (for immediate context), a ticket (for team visibility), or an ADR (for long-term record).

**The open question:** What is the decision rule for where a specific piece of information belongs? For example: a decision made during the session whose rationale is needed by the next agent immediately — does it go in the handoff, the ticket, the ADR, or some combination? What degrades fastest and therefore belongs in the handoff vs. what ages well and therefore belongs in a durable artifact?
